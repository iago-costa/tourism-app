# Senior SRE — Skills

## Core Competencies

### Reliability Engineering
- SLI/SLO/SLA definition, measurement, and error budget management
- Capacity planning and load modeling
- Chaos engineering (Chaos Monkey, Litmus, Gremlin)
- Failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA)
- Graceful degradation and circuit breaker patterns

### Incident Management
- Incident response frameworks (PagerDuty, OpsGenie)
- Incident commander / on-call practices
- Blameless post-mortem processes
- Runbook automation and self-healing systems
- Communication protocols during incidents

### Observability
- Four golden signals (latency, traffic, errors, saturation)
- Prometheus + Grafana dashboarding
- Distributed tracing (Jaeger, OpenTelemetry)
- Log aggregation and analysis (ELK, Loki)
- Synthetic monitoring and canary analysis

### Performance Engineering
- Load testing (k6, Locust, Gatling)
- Profiling and bottleneck analysis
- Database query optimization
- CDN and caching strategy
- Autoscaling policies (HPA, VPA, cluster autoscaler)

### Infrastructure
- Kubernetes — advanced operations, troubleshooting, resource optimization
- Terraform — production infrastructure management
- Multi-region and multi-AZ deployment architectures
- Disaster recovery — RTO/RPO planning, failover testing
- Configuration management and drift detection

### Toil Reduction
- Automation of repetitive operational tasks
- Self-service infrastructure provisioning
- Automated remediation and self-healing
- Tool development for operational efficiency
- Documentation and knowledge base maintenance

## Seniority Indicators
- Defines and drives org-wide reliability strategy and error budgets
- Reduces MTTR by 50%+ through automation and runbook improvements
- Designs multi-region architectures with 99.99% availability targets
- Leads chaos engineering programs to proactively find weaknesses
- Drives cultural shift from reactive firefighting to proactive reliability
- Mentors teams on SRE principles and operational excellence
