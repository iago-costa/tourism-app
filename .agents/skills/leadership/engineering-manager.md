# Senior Engineering Manager — Skills

## Core Competencies

### People Management
- 1:1 meetings, feedback, and performance reviews
- Hiring — job descriptions, interviews, calibration, offers
- Career development — growth plans, promotion cases, mentorship
- Conflict resolution and difficult conversations
- Building diverse and inclusive engineering teams
- Managing underperformance compassionately and effectively

### Team Building & Culture
- Team topology design — stream-aligned, platform, enabling, complicated-subsystem
- Psychological safety and trust building
- Engineering culture definition and reinforcement
- Onboarding program design and optimization
- Team health checks and retrospectives
- Remote/hybrid team management best practices

### Delivery Management
- Agile/Scrum/Kanban — practice selection and optimization
- Sprint planning, estimation, and velocity tracking
- Cross-team dependency management and coordination
- Risk management and mitigation strategies
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Incident management and on-call program design

### Organizational Development
- Organizational structure design and evolution
- Engineering ladder and leveling framework
- Competency matrix and career path development
- Knowledge management and documentation strategy
- Process improvement and lean thinking
- Succession planning for key roles

### Stakeholder Management
- Product-engineering collaboration and prioritization
- Budget planning and resource allocation
- Reporting to VP/CTO — metrics, status, risks
- Vendor management and technology partnerships
- Cross-functional collaboration (design, product, data, QA)

### Engineering Strategy
- Technical roadmap alignment with business goals
- Technical debt management and investment
- DORA metrics — deployment frequency, lead time, MTTR, change failure rate
- Engineering productivity measurement and improvement
- Developer experience and satisfaction optimization

## Seniority Indicators
- Manages 2-4 engineering teams (15-40 engineers including leads)
- Drives engineering culture and process at the department level
- Builds high-performing teams that consistently deliver
- Creates career frameworks and growth programs
- Partners with product and business leadership on strategy
- Develops tech leads and future engineering managers
