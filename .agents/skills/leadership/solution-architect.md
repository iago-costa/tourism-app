# Senior Solution Architect — Skills

## Core Competencies

### Solution Design
- End-to-end solution architecture for complex business problems
- Integration architecture — connecting disparate enterprise systems
- Cloud-native solution design (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- Multi-tier application architecture (web, mobile, API, data)
- Architecture patterns — microservices, event-driven, serverless, hybrid

### Requirements Engineering
- Business requirement analysis and decomposition
- Non-functional requirements — scalability, performance, security, compliance
- Gap analysis between current state and target architecture
- Feasibility studies and proof-of-concept design
- Architecture trade-off analysis (ATAM)

### Stakeholder Communication
- Architecture presentations to C-suite audience
- Technical proposal writing for RFPs/RFIs
- Workshop facilitation — architecture discovery and design sessions
- ROI and TCO analysis for technology investments
- Vendor evaluation and technology recommendation reports

### Technology Landscape
- Enterprise systems — ERP, CRM, HCM integration
- Cloud platforms — deep knowledge of AWS/GCP/Azure services
- Data platforms — warehousing, analytics, AI/ML integration
- Security architecture — IAM, encryption, compliance frameworks
- DevOps — CI/CD, IaC, monitoring, containerization

### Architecture Governance
- Architecture review boards and approval processes
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Reference architecture creation and maintenance
- Technology standards and compliance
- Technical risk register management

### Pre-Sales & Consulting
- Solution scoping and estimation
- Proof-of-concept development and demo
- Client relationship management
- Architecture workshops and training
- Post-implementation review and optimization

## Seniority Indicators
- Designs solutions spanning 10+ systems and multiple domains
- Wins multi-million dollar engagements with architecture proposals
- Creates reference architectures adopted across the organization
- Mentors junior architects and solution engineers
- Influences technology strategy at the enterprise level
- Translates complex technical concepts for business stakeholders fluently
