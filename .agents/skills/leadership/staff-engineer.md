# Senior Staff Engineer — Skills

## Core Competencies

### Technical Strategy
- Organization-wide technology strategy and vision
- Multi-year technical roadmap creation and alignment
- Build vs buy analysis at enterprise scale
- Technology radar — emerging tech evaluation and adoption strategy
- Technical investment portfolio management (features vs platform vs debt)

### System Architecture
- Distributed system design at massive scale
- Cross-service and cross-team architecture design
- Platform and infrastructure architecture
- Data architecture and data flow design
- Migration strategies for large-scale system evolution

### Technical Influence
- Defining engineering standards and best practices org-wide
- Influencing without authority across teams and organizations
- Building consensus on contentious technical decisions
- RFC/ADR process ownership and review
- Technical mentorship for senior and staff-level engineers

### Cross-Team Impact
- Identifying and driving cross-cutting technical initiatives
- Removing systemic technical impediments
- Cross-team code review and architecture review
- Organizational technical debt programs
- Shared infrastructure and platform development

### Communication & Leadership
- Executive-level technical communication
- Written technical communication — vision documents, strategy papers
- Conference speaking and thought leadership
- Open-source strategy and community engagement
- Engineering blog and knowledge sharing programs

### Business Acumen
- Understanding business metrics and how technology enables them
- Product-engineering alignment and collaboration
- Cost optimization at organizational scale
- Vendor relationship and contract negotiation
- Competitive technical analysis

## Seniority Indicators
- Influences engineering direction across 50+ engineers
- Designs systems serving millions of users with 99.99% availability
- Defines and drives multi-year technical strategy for the organization
- Creates engineering standards adopted across all teams
- Mentors tech leads and senior engineers on leadership
- Acts as the technical conscience of the engineering organization
