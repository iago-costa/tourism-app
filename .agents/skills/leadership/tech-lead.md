# Senior Tech Lead — Skills

## Core Competencies

### Technical Leadership
- Architectural decision-making and trade-off analysis
- Technical vision and roadmap creation
- Technology evaluation and selection
- Technical debt management and prioritization
- Code review culture and quality standards
- RFC/ADR processes for technical decisions

### Team Enablement
- Engineering team mentorship and growth planning
- Pair programming and knowledge sharing
- Onboarding process design and improvement
- Technical interview design and calibration
- Career development conversations and promotion support
- Creating psychological safety for experimentation

### Delivery & Execution
- Sprint planning and capacity management
- Technical risk identification and mitigation
- Cross-team dependency management
- Incident response leadership and post-mortem facilitation
- Release management and deployment strategy
- Technical scope negotiation with product

### Architecture & Design
- System design for scale, reliability, and maintainability
- API design and service boundary definition
- Database schema design and data modeling
- Performance optimization and capacity planning
- Security architecture review
- Observability and monitoring strategy

### Communication
- Technical writing — RFCs, ADRs, design documents, postmortems
- Stakeholder management — translating technical concepts for non-technical audiences
- Cross-team alignment and coordination
- Presenting technical strategy to leadership
- Running effective technical design reviews

### Process & Culture
- Engineering process improvement (CI/CD, testing, code review)
- DevOps culture and collaboration promotion
- Innovation time and hackathon organization
- Technical community building (guilds, book clubs, tech talks)
- Open-source contribution strategy

## Seniority Indicators
- Leads teams of 5-15 engineers with autonomous delivery capability
- Drives architectural decisions with multi-year impact
- Balances technical excellence with business delivery pragmatically
- Unblocks teams by resolving ambiguity and making decisions
- Grows engineers from junior to senior through active mentorship
- Communicates technical strategy effectively to VP/C-level audiences
