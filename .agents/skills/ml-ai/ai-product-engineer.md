# Senior AI Product Engineer — Skills

## Core Competencies

### AI Product Integration
- LLM integration — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, open-source models
- RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) architecture
- Conversational AI — chatbots, copilots, assistants
- AI-powered search and recommendations
- Content generation — text, code, images, summaries

### Prompt Engineering
- System prompts, few-shot learning, chain-of-thought
- Prompt templating and versioning
- Output parsing and structured generation (JSON mode, function calling)
- Prompt evaluation and optimization
- Guardrails and safety filters

### Application Architecture
- LangChain, LlamaIndex, Semantic Kernel — application frameworks
- Vector databases — Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, ChromaDB, pgvector
- Embedding pipelines — chunking strategies, embedding models
- Caching strategies for AI responses (semantic caching)
- Streaming responses and real-time AI interactions

### Evaluation & Quality
- LLM evaluation frameworks — RAGAS, DeepEval, custom evals
- Human evaluation workflows
- Automated testing for AI features
- Hallucination detection and mitigation
- Response quality metrics and benchmarking

### User Experience
- AI-native UX patterns (copilots, suggestions, autocomplete)
- Confidence indicators and uncertainty communication
- Human-in-the-loop workflows
- Progressive disclosure of AI capabilities
- Feedback loops for model improvement

### Ethics & Safety
- Content moderation and safety filters
- Bias detection in AI outputs
- Privacy in AI applications (data handling, PII)
- Responsible AI product design
- Transparency and explainability for end users

## Seniority Indicators
- Designs AI-powered product features used by millions of users
- Creates RAG architectures that achieve >90% answer relevance
- Defines AI product strategy and evaluation frameworks
- Builds AI feature platforms reusable across multiple products
- Mentors product and engineering teams on AI integration
- Drives responsible AI practices in product development
